##  Tsunami Preparedness & Safety Steps

### **Step 1: Pay Attention to Warnings**
- Always listen for tsunami warnings, sirens, or emergency alerts.
- Don't ignore warnings or wait for it to happen.

---

### **Step 2: Evacuate Immediately**
- Move away from the coast as soon as possible.
- Do not wait or try to watch the waves.
- Go to higher ground right away.

---

### **Step 3: Know Your Safe Zone**
- Safe zones are high ground areas away from the ocean.
- Learn tsunami evacuation routes ahead of time, know the safe zones and plan accordingly

---

### **Step 4: Practice a Tsunami Plan**
- Have a plan with your family.
- Everyone should know where to go and what to do.
- Practice evacuation routes.
- Know the drill so your not lost in emergency.

---

### **Step 5: Have a Backup Plan (Plan B)**
- Your main route may be blocked.
- Always have another safe path planned.

---

### **Step 6: Go Bag**
- Keep an emergency bag ready.
- Include:
  - Water
  - Flashlight
  - First aid kit
  - Snacks
  - Important documents

---

### **Step 7: Bring Enough Supplies**
- Have enough supplies for at least 3 days.
- Supplies should be lightweight and easy to carry.
- You need enough supplies to stay safe until you can be rescued.

---

### **Step 8: Stay in Safe Zones**
- Stay in the safe zone until officials say it is safe.
- Do not return too early.
- Don't go to check on property or other things until it is clear.
---

### **Step 9: Stay Positive**
- Stay calm and help others.
- A positive mindset helps during emergencies.
- Don't freak out cause you will make the most mistakes when your not calm.

---

### **Step 10: Share Your Plan**
- Share your tsunami plan with others.
- This helps everyone stay prepared.
- It can also get your plan improved with others opinions.
