Hey there! Welcome to my portfolio. I'm Manvir Singh, a Junior from Huron who has always been fascinated by how technology works. That curiosity led me straight to my favorite class: Webpage Design, which I first took in my freshman year. I have always been fascinated by technology, and before I took this class, I had tried learning about coding, but for C++ for like Arduino. So when I got to high school, I was excited to take Webpage Design. This class has been a great way for me to learn more about coding and web design, and I've really enjoyed the projects we've done so far. I'm looking forward to learning even more in this class and seeing where my interest in technology takes me in the future.

Away from the keyboard, I'm just a regular guy who values time with my family and hanging out with my friends. I wanted my portfolio website to feel personal and inviting, which is why I chose a warm and friendly theme. Think of this site as my digital scrapbook and workshop, a place where I get to combine my personal interests with the new skills I'm picking up in class. I hope you enjoy exploring it as much as I enjoyed building it
